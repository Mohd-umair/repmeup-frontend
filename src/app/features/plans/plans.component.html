Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change plans anytime?

Yes! You can upgrade your plan at any time. Your new limits take effect immediately.

What happens if I reach my limit?

When you reach your account limit, you'll be prompted to upgrade. Your existing connections will continue working normally.

Do you offer refunds?

Refund eligibility depends on your plan and when you request it (Annual plans have a limited window; Monthly plans are non-refundable). See the Refund & Cancellation Policy for the binding terms.

What payment methods do you accept?

Payments are processed securely via Razorpay. You can pay with UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, and popular wallets.

1. Nature and characterisation of services

1.1 Platform overview

The Platform is an AI-powered, SaaS-based digital solution that enables Users to automate and manage customer communications across major social media and messaging platforms, including but not limited to Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, and YouTube. The Platform leverages proprietary artificial intelligence models and large language model (LLM) integrations trained on User-uploaded knowledge bases to generate brand-aligned responses in real time.

1.2 Revenue optimisation function

The Platform is designed to help Users monetise and maximise commercial value from their social media presence by ensuring prompt, contextually accurate, and commercially oriented responses to comments, direct messages (DMs), story replies, and other engagement touchpoints. The Platform's primary commercial purpose is to drive conversion, retention, and revenue for Users' businesses.

1.3 Instant consumption and digital delivery

The services provided under this Platform are:

  • Digital, intangible, and on-demand in nature;
  • Delivered electronically and consumed immediately upon activation;
  • Non-reversible once provisioned, as AI model deployment, knowledge base processing, API integrations, and system configurations are executed instantly and cannot be undone.

For the purposes of applicable Indian law, including the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, and by analogy to comparable digital subscription frameworks, the services constitute a "digital product" or "digital service" the delivery and consumption of which commences immediately upon purchase. Accordingly, standard return or refund rights applicable to physical goods are expressly not applicable.

1.4 Subscription-based access

All access to the Platform is governed by a prepaid, time-bound subscription. Upon payment, the User is immediately granted access to the full suite of features applicable to their chosen plan. The Company incurs real, immediate, and irreversible costs upon activation, including compute resources, API gateway charges, AI model inference costs, third-party integration fees, and infrastructure provisioning expenses.

Refunds in respect of the Annual Plan are subject to the strict conditions, timelines, and deductions set forth below. The mere passage of a request does not entitle a User to a refund; all conditions must be simultaneously and fully satisfied.

4.1 Window 1: Full refund (Days 1–15 from purchase date)

A User may be considered eligible for a refund of up to one hundred percent (100%) of the subscription fee paid, net of all applicable deductions under Clause 5, provided that:

  • The refund request is submitted within fifteen (15) calendar days from the date of purchase ("Purchase Date");

  • The request is verified and approved by the Company in its sole and absolute discretion;
  • All conditions in Clause 5 are fully satisfied at the time of the request.

Important: Satisfaction of the 15-day time window alone does not guarantee a refund. Eligibility remains subject to usage assessment, deductions, and Company approval.

4.2 Window 2: Partial refund (Days 16–30 from purchase date)

If a refund request is submitted after fifteen (15) calendar days but within thirty (30) calendar days from the Purchase Date, the User may be eligible for a maximum refund of fifty percent (50%) of the subscription fee paid, net of all deductions under Clause 5, subject to:

  • Full compliance with all submission requirements in Clause 4.1(b)–(f) above;
  • The Company's sole and absolute discretion regarding approval and amount;
  • A proportionate reduction in refund quantum based on actual Platform usage during the period.

4.3 No refund beyond 30 days

Absolutely no refund, credit, or other remedy shall be available for any refund request submitted thirty (30) or more calendar days after the Purchase Date, regardless of:

Upon the expiry of thirty (30) days from the Purchase Date, the entire subscription fee is deemed fully earned by the Company, and the User irrevocably waives any right to claim a refund thereof.

D. Data Deletion Requests:

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data from RepMeUp. We provide multiple methods to request data deletion:

Method 1: Through Facebook/Instagram (Recommended)

If you connected your RepMeUp account using Facebook or Instagram, you can request data deletion directly through Meta's platform:

  1. Go to your Facebook Settings at facebook.com/settings?tab=applications
  2. Navigate to "Apps and Websites" section
  3. Find "RepMeUp" in your list of connected apps
  4. Click "Remove" next to RepMeUp
  5. Click "View Removed Apps and Websites" link
  6. Click "View" next to RepMeUp
  7. Click "Send Request" to initiate data deletion
  8. You will receive a confirmation code to track your deletion request
  9. Visit our Data Deletion Status page with your confirmation code to check the progress

Note: This method is compliant with Meta's Platform Terms and provides immediate confirmation of your deletion request.

Method 2: Direct Email Request

You can also request data deletion by sending an email to our privacy team:

  • Email: privacy@repmeup.in
  • Subject Line: "Data Deletion Request"
  • Include: Your account email address, full name, and reason for deletion (optional)

We will process your request within 30 days and send you a confirmation email with a tracking code.

What Data Gets Deleted?

When you request data deletion, we will permanently remove:

  • Your account information (name, email, profile data)
  • Platform connections (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, etc.)
  • All interactions (posts, comments, messages, replies)
  • Access tokens and authentication credentials
  • Platform-specific user IDs
  • Conversation threads and sentiment analysis data

Note: Some data may be retained for legal, accounting, or audit purposes as required by law (e.g., payment records for 7 years). Aggregated, anonymized analytics data may also be retained.

Deletion Timeline

  • Immediate: Your deletion request is acknowledged and a confirmation code is generated
  • Within 24 hours: Platform connections are deactivated and access tokens revoked
  • Within 7 days: All personal data and interactions are permanently deleted from our active systems
  • Within 30 days: Data is removed from backup systems (except where legally required to retain)

You can track the status of your deletion request using your confirmation code at any time on our Data Deletion Status page.
